- **Grading rules, in one paragraph:** the typed reading is normalized to hiragana and compared with the primary reading and every alternate whose status is not `nonstandard`; a hit on the primary is 正解, a hit on an alternate is 別読みで正解 with that alternate's status shown. Otherwise, for a decomposable entry, the typed string is segmented over the characters: each character offers its recorded reading plus every on and kun inventory reading (kun readings also as their 連用形 and い-adjective stems), each with the 連濁, 半濁音化 and 促音 variants applied; the segmentation covering the whole string with the highest score wins (exact 4, phonetic 3, inventory 2, classification 1 per character). A character whose segment is from the other type's inventory is a 分類の誤り; the same type but a different inventory reading is a 読みの誤り; the same inventory reading in a different phonetic form (missing or over-applied) is a 音変化の誤り naming the change; the overall cause is the first present in that order. No covering segmentation is 部分一致なし. 熟字訓 entries are whole-word: correct or 熟字訓の誤り, no analysis, and the reveal says why decomposition fails.
